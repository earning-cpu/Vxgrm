# Fact-Check Dossier — The Dancing Plague of 1518 (Strasbourg)

**Purpose:** Source-of-truth for the YouTube Short script. Every load-bearing claim in the video was checked against multiple independent sources. Claims that are disputed are flagged and phrased cautiously in the script ("some chronicles claim…", "historians disagree").

**Topic chosen because:** it is (1) real and documented, (2) unsolved to this day, (3) folkloric/religious in flavor (curse of St. Vitus), (4) deeply visual and horrific, (5) ideal for a 9:16 horror Short.

## 3. Explicitly NOT claimed in the video (to avoid misinformation)
- Supernatural causation (framed as mystery/legend, never as fact).
- A precise death toll (always hedged).
- That "400 died" (400 is the *participant* estimate, not deaths).
- Modern medical certainty of any theory.

## 5. Verdict
The event is **real and well documented**; the **cause remains unsolved**. Ideal "true story, unexplained" horror material. Using hedged language keeps the video factually honest while preserving dread.
